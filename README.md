# Texts From Your Future Self — You From Next Tuesday

One funny text message from your future self, sent back to the present self: your future self (only a few days ahead, next Tuesday) has read all 5,271 reminder messages you sent yourself about finishing something, replied to every one of them — and neither of you is done.
